## 2. What is directly measured vs estimated
====================================================================================================
  DIRECTLY MEASURED (by this project): Delhi-wide 5-min SLDC demand, resampled to 15-min --
  this is the ONLY demand quantity this project actually observes.
  REPORTED (by third parties, not measured by this project): the MW figures in the source table
  above -- each is a same-utility, dated news or company report; not independently verified by
  us, and treated as data, not ground truth.
  MODELED ESTIMATE (computed by this script): every per-DISCOM number attached to our own
  forecast below. These are the Delhi-wide P50/P90 multiplied by a disclosed ratio -- NOT an
  independent per-DISCOM forecast, and NEVER to be described as 'live' or 'measured'.

PRE-FLIGHT CHECK: locked pipeline reproduced 5,817 test blocks, Delhi P50 at the known
season peak = 7,795.2 MW (P90 8,417.6) -- identical to Phase 7-10. Nothing about the demand model changes in this phase.

====================================================================================================
## 3. Recommended localization method
====================================================================================================
  METHOD B: a disclosed allocation-ratio approach derived from credible, dated, reported
  record-day figures (Section 1), applied by simple proportional scaling to our own Delhi-wide
  P50/P90 forecast. This is NOT an independent per-DISCOM model -- there is no per-DISCOM
  training data to fit one. Every per-DISCOM number below is explicitly a MODELED ESTIMATE.

====================================================================================================
## 4. Ratio derivation
====================================================================================================
  BRPL share, 2025-06-12 (same-day actual, same date as our dataset's peak): 3,747/8,423 = 44.49%
  BYPL share, 2025-06-12 (same-day actual): 1,832/8,423 = 21.75%
  BRPL share, 2026-05-21 (season-to-date, independent later date): 3,762/8,231 = 45.71%
  BYPL share, 2026-05-21 (season-to-date): 1,838/8,231 = 22.33%
  TPDDL share, 2026-05-21 (season-to-date -- the ONLY dated TPDDL figure found; no 2025-06-12
  TPDDL actual could be located despite search): 2,331/8,231 = 28.32%

  CHOSEN RATIOS (used for all forecasts below):
    BRPL                 44.49%
    BYPL                 21.75%
    TPDDL                28.32%
    NDMC_MES_RESIDUAL     5.44%
  BRPL and BYPL use the 2025-06-12 SAME-DAY figures (most relevant to our forecast period).
  TPDDL uses the only sourced figure available (2026-05-21, a different year) -- flagged as a
  weaker, single-source estimate throughout this report, not equal-confidence to BRPL/BYPL.
  NDMC + MES: the residual after BRPL+BYPL+TPDDL (5.44%) is
  carried as a SINGLE undecomposed bucket. No individual NDMC or MES number is produced --
  no sourced data exists to split this residual credibly.

====================================================================================================
## 5. Consistency checks
====================================================================================================
  Do the shares approximately sum to 100%? BRPL+BYPL+TPDDL = 94.56%, leaving 5.44% for NDMC+MES -- plausible: NDMC (Lutyens' Delhi, a small
  area) and MES (Delhi Cantonment, defence-administered, often outside standard DERC-regulated
  DISCOM reporting) are both widely understood to be minor relative to the three main DISCOMs;
  a single-digit combined residual is directionally consistent with that, though not itself
  independently verified.
  Same total-demand definition? Both source dates report an SLDC-measured citywide peak MW
  figure, the same basis our own Delhi-wide model is trained on (SLDC-derived demand) -- yes.
  Comparable dates? BRPL/BYPL: 2025-06-12 is the SAME DAY as our project's own known season
  peak -- as comparable as a date can be. TPDDL: 2026-05-21 is a different YEAR (though the
  same pre-monsoon summer season) -- a real, disclosed limitation, not hidden.
  Reconcilable utilities? BRPL and BYPL are cross-checked below (Section 6) against an
  independent date and reconcile within ~3%. TPDDL has only one source and cannot be
  cross-checked this way. NDMC/MES cannot be reconciled at all -- no data exists for either.

====================================================================================================
## 6. Historical sanity check
====================================================================================================
  The BRPL/BYPL ratios above were derived ONLY from 2025-06-12. Here they are applied, WITHOUT
  any adjustment, to the INDEPENDENT 2026-05-21 total (8,231 MW) and compared against that day's
  OWN separately reported BRPL/BYPL figures -- an honest out-of-sample check, not a circular one:
    BRPL: ratio (from 6/12/25) x 8,231 MW = 3,662 MW predicted vs 3,762 MW actually reported on 5/21/26  ->  error -2.7%
    BYPL: ratio (from 6/12/25) x 8,231 MW = 1,790 MW predicted vs 1,838 MW actually reported on 5/21/26  ->  error -2.6%
  Both within +-3%, roughly a year apart, on genuinely independent data -- reasonable evidence
  the BRPL/BYPL ratios are stable, though this is ONE cross-check on ONE pair of dates, not a
  large validated sample. No threshold or ratio was adjusted after seeing this result.
  TPDDL cannot be sanity-checked this way -- only one dated figure exists for it, and it was
  used to derive the ratio itself, so there is no independent data left to test against.

## 8. Contribution vs relative-risk interpretation
====================================================================================================
  CONTRIBUTION = the fixed allocation share (Section 4) -- by construction, BRPL is always the
  largest contributor (44.5% of every modeled block) and always will be, since contribution is a
  static ratio, not a forecast quantity.
  RELATIVE STRESS = modeled demand vs that DISCOM's OWN stated 2025 reference peak -- this DOES
  vary block to block, because it depends on the Delhi-wide forecast level, not just the ratio.
  At the main peak event above:
    BRPL: contribution 44.5% of Delhi's modeled peak, but relative stress (P50 vs own 2025 reference) = 86%
    BYPL: contribution 21.8% of Delhi's modeled peak, but relative stress (P50 vs own 2025 reference) = 89%
    TPDDL: contribution 28.3% of Delhi's modeled peak, but relative stress (P50 vs own 2025 reference) = 86%
  Ranked by CONTRIBUTION: BRPL > TPDDL > BYPL
  Ranked by RELATIVE STRESS: BYPL > TPDDL > BRPL
  These rankings DIFFER: BRPL carries the largest absolute modeled load, but
  BYPL is modeled as proportionally closer to what it itself said it expected
  this summer -- exactly the 'largest share is not the same as highest relative risk'
  distinction this phase was asked to preserve. This emerged from the sourced numbers, not
  from the design of the measure.

====================================================================================================
## 9. Credibility limitations
====================================================================================================
  - BRPL and BYPL ratios rest on ONE same-day actual (2025-06-12) plus ONE independent
    out-of-sample check (2026-05-21) -- two data points, not a validated time series. A single
    unusual day could bias the ratio; we have no way to detect that with only two dates.
  - TPDDL's ratio rests on a SINGLE source, from a different year than our forecast period, with
    NO independent cross-check available. It is materially weaker evidence than BRPL/BYPL and is
    labeled as such everywhere it appears.
  - NDMC and MES have ZERO individual sourced data. Their combined residual share is an
    arithmetic leftover, not a reported or modeled quantity in its own right -- it could equally
    represent NDMC and MES, or unreconciled measurement differences between the two total-demand
    sources (SLDC same-day vs SLDC season-to-date), or simple rounding in the news reports.
  - The allocation ratio is a FIXED proportion applied uniformly across all 15-min blocks, all
    hours, and both weekday/weekend. There is no evidence in this project that DISCOM shares are
    actually constant across time of day or season -- this is an assumption of the method, not a
    demonstrated fact. A DISCOM with more commercial (daytime) load than residential (evening)
    load, for example, would violate this assumption and this method would not detect it.
  - The 'relative stress' reference (own 2025 stated expected peak) is each utility's OWN
    forecast of its own demand, not an engineering or contractual capacity limit. A DISCOM
    exceeding its own earlier forecast is not the same as approaching an actual grid constraint.
  - No DISCOM-level uncertainty (P10/P90 spread specific to that DISCOM) exists -- the P50/P90
    shown per DISCOM is just the Delhi-wide band scaled by the same fixed ratio, which assumes
    each DISCOM's forecast uncertainty is proportional to Delhi's -- untested.

====================================================================================================
## 10. Should this feature be included in the final product?
====================================================================================================
  DECISION: YES, WITH CLEAR 'MODELED ESTIMATE' LABEL -- scoped to BRPL, BYPL, and TPDDL only.
  NDMC and MES are shown ONLY as a combined, explicitly undecomposed residual, never as
  individual figures.
  Why YES rather than NO: two of the three included DISCOMs (BRPL, BYPL) have a same-day sourced
  figure from the exact date of our own dataset's known peak, plus a genuine, non-circular
  out-of-sample check that reconciled within 3%. That clears a real bar of credibility -- this
  is not a guess.
  Why WITH LABEL and not an unqualified YES: TPDDL rests on a single, differently-dated source;
  NDMC/MES have no source at all; and the method assumes time-invariant shares, which is not
  demonstrated. Every one of these must stay visible to whoever uses this feature, not buried in
  a methodology note.
  Why not NO: declining to build anything would discard two DISCOMs' worth of genuinely
  cross-validated, dated, sourced evidence -- the credibility bar in the brief is 'defensible',
  not 'perfect', and BRPL/BYPL clear it.
