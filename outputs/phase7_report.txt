PART D -- GRID STRESS SCORE (transparent, documented, NOT a learned target)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  assumed capacity : 9,000 MW
  ASSUMED / ILLUSTRATIVE ceiling, not an official or sourced figure. Chosen as a round value above the highest demand observed anywhere in our dataset (8,631.5 MW at 5-min resolution). It exists to demonstrate the index and MUST be replaced with a verified operational value before any external claim.
  score = 55*u(P50) + 20*u(P90) + 10*growth + 10*heat + 5*unreliability   (max 100)
    u(x)      = clip((x/capacity - 0.7) / (1.0 - 0.7), 0, 1)
    growth    = clip(day-over-day change in forecast peak / 0.08, 0, 1)
    heat      = clip((corrected forecast temp - 30.0) / (42.0 - 30.0), 0, 1)
    unreliab. = HIGH 0.0, MEDIUM 0.5, LOW 1.0
